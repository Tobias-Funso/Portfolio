The Lowest-Cost-Per-Purchase Ad

ObjectiveDrive purchases at the lowest possible cost across a live e-commerce account.
Challenge21 ad variations running at once, most absorbing budget with zero purchases.
StrategyIsolated which ad was actually converting, then prioritized its budget.
AudienceMeta's automatic placements across Facebook & Instagram, optimized for on-site purchases.
Results₦428,960.22 in spend produced 46 purchases at ₦9,325.22 each — the most efficient cost-per-purchase of any ad in the account.

The Ad That Carried The Account

ObjectiveIdentify which of 21 live ads was actually worth scaling further.
