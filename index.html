A look inside two real ads from a live 21-ad Meta Ads account — the strategy behind them and the results they delivered.

Meta Ads · E-commerce

The lowest-cost-per-purchase ad in the account

ObjectiveDrive purchases at the lowest possible cost across a live e-commerce Meta Ads account
Challenge21 ad variations were running at once, most absorbing budget with zero purchases to show for it
StrategyIsolated which specific ad was actually converting, then prioritized its budget over the underperforming variants
AudienceMeta's automatic placements across Facebook & Instagram, optimized for on-site purchases
Creative ApproachSingle-image product ad, kept in rotation long enough to reach a 2.42 frequency without fatiguing
What Was TestedThis creative against 20 other live variations in the same ad set over a 4-month window
Results₦428,960.22 in spend produced 46 purchases at ₦9,325.22 each — the most efficient cost-per-purchase of any ad in the account

Meta Ads · E-commerce

The single ad that carried the account

ObjectiveIdentify which of 21 live ads was actually worth scaling further
ChallengeAccount-wide spend was climbing, but purchases were concentrated in a small number of ads
StrategyTracked landing page views alongside purchases to confirm this ad wasn't just getting clicks — it was converting them
AudienceSame Meta automatic placement setup as the rest of the account, run concurrently for a fair comparison
Creative ApproachHeld a 1.97 frequency while still driving the highest landing page view count of any ad tested
What Was TestedPerformance against 20 other ad variations in the same campaign over the same date range
Results₦618,495.87 in spend produced 59 purchases — over a third of the entire account's total purchases from one ad

What keeps me hooked on marketing isn't just the campaigns — it's the constant learning. Every algorithm shift, every new ad format, every AI tool that lands is another puzzle to solve, and I genuinely enjoy solving it.

Beyond the metrics, I care about growing — as a marketer and as a person. Every project is a chance to learn something new, sharpen a skill, and bring a little more creativity into the next campaign.
